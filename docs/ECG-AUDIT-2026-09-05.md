# ECG review — 5 September 2026

Local implementation audit. **Calibrated means consistent SVG time/voltage geometry, not clinical validation.** All examples remain synthetic teaching material. No patient recordings were imported and no clinician has signed off their diagnostic morphology.

## Verified corrections

- Connected schematic beats and removed backward tracing segments and T/U overlap with the next beat. Paper grid now shares SVG coordinates; footnotes wrap.
- Corrected augmented limb-lead scaling and made the inferior example obey Einthoven/Goldberger relationships. Preserved 25 mm/s, 10 mm/mV and existing conversion helpers.
- de Winter now has continuously upsloping depression in V2–V6 with distinct tall T waves; posterior V1–V3 have horizontal depression and broader dominant R waves. Wellens A/B now exist in anatomical V2/V3 as well as the original comparison aliases.
- Added AF with genuinely irregular ventricular intervals and no organized P waves. Clean mode retains the fibrillatory signal. VT capture/fusion use the existing independent atrial train; complete block retains independent atrial/ventricular timing.
- Schematic Brugada now descends into a negative T wave, TCA has a terminal R after the main negative aVR complex, pericarditis includes PR displacement, and the WPW delta is continuous. Expanded the visible lead distribution for diffuse depression and RV strain.
- Removed absolute “confirms infarction” and “any criterion = OMI” wording from case annotations. Dynamic findings identify relevant modeled leads/segments; Normal comparison is labeled as a synthetic normal model.
