# Oncology Emergencies · 13 November 2026

The Library includes six adult guides under the Oncology Emergencies clinical
system. Each has its own route and searchable teaching, with related links from
the relevant symptom pages. The app keeps all clinical text available offline.

- Febrile neutropenia and neutropenic sepsis covers immediate empiric treatment,
  afebrile presentations, focal complications and specialist-led disposition.
  Sources include NICE CG151, ASCO/IDSA and AGIHO/DGHO.
- Tumor lysis syndrome covers the laboratory pattern, acute kidney injury,
  arrhythmia, monitoring and rasburicase safety. Sources include BSH 2025,
  MD Anderson and eviQ.
- Metastatic spinal cord compression covers neurological warning signs,
  emergency MRI and the NICE NG234 dexamethasone regimen.
- Hypercalcemia of malignancy covers volume assessment, antiresorptive therapy
  and short-term calcitonin for severe disease, following the Endocrine Society.
- Superior vena cava syndrome covers airway assessment, contrast imaging and
  escalation, drawing on ACR and NHS Ayrshire & Arran.
- Cancer immunotherapy emergencies covers CAR-T toxicities, concurrent sepsis
  assessment and serious checkpoint inhibitor reactions, drawing on SITC.

The clinical references and review scope are recorded in `assets/evidence.js`.
Antibiotic selection, rasburicase and immunotherapy toxicity treatment depend on
local protocols and the treating team. The MSCC dose row is scoped to patients
with neurological symptoms or signs.
