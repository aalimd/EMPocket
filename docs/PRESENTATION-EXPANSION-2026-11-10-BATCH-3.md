# Selected presentation expansion, batch 3 — 10 November 2026

Scope: 32 additional teaching points across eight further presentations, with
selected corrections in the reviewed topics. There are now 24 presentations
with scoped additions reviewed on this date. This is not a complete clinical
audit of those topics or the 52-presentation application. Dose tables are unchanged.

- Eye emergencies: AAO EyeWiki chemical-injury assessment and irrigation;
  existing AAO keratitis guidance. Exposure history, delayed pH recheck, retained
  particles and open-globe precautions. The conflicting suggestion that negative
  fluorescein excludes ulcer/infection is removed.
- Sore throat: CDC GAS guidance. Viral features, age-dependent test follow-up,
  appearance limitations and transmission advice. Airway assessment retains priority.
- Scrotal pain: CDC epididymitis guidance. Testing, ultrasound limitations,
  response assessment and STI aftercare. These additions do not replace urgent
  torsion assessment or expand antibiotic doses.
- Diarrhea: IDSA infectious-diarrhea guidance. Selective testing, interpreting
  panels, oral rehydration, feeding and persistent symptoms. Disposition specifies
  CDI needing inpatient care rather than implying admission for every CDI case.
- Rash: IDSA SSTI guidance. Exposure risks, purulent/nonpurulent patterns,
  selective cultures and recurrence prevention. Itch is no longer described as
  establishing a benign cause.
- Acute limb ischemia: ACC/AHA multisociety PAD guideline (2024). Timed
  examination, Doppler findings, urgent treatment priority and reperfusion surveillance.
- Urinary retention: AUA urotrauma guidance and NHS catheter-care information.
  Suspected urethral injury needs specialist-directed evaluation; discharge
  teaching covers practical care, warning signs and contact/follow-up arrangements.
- Head injury: NICE NG232, ACEP adult mTBI policy (2023), IDSA basilar-fracture
  prophylaxis recommendation. Selected CT eligibility/timing summaries are
  qualified. Adult minor injury with reassuring CT and baseline neurology does
  not automatically require admission/repeat CT solely for antithrombotic use;
  other indications still govern care. Basilar fracture/CSF leak alone does not
  justify preventive antibiotics; open/penetrating injury is a separate indication
  assessment. Advice covers baseline function, supervision and persistent symptoms. The
  unsupported superlative about a motor-GCS drop is replaced with an escalation prompt.

Exact titles/URLs and scope are in `assets/evidence.js`. New keys:
`chemicalEye`, `infectiousDiarrhea`, `urethralTrauma`, `catheterCare`, `mtbi`,
`csfLeak`. The historical full-review date remains unchanged. Society education
and patient-care instructions are labelled as such, not presented as new guidelines.
Some primary publisher pages required indexed extracts or the society's official
policy publication; unsupported claims were not added.

Validation: source and safety regressions, search destinations, phone-browser
checks across all 24 expanded presentations, full Node suite, syntax and release
consistency. These checks establish implementation behavior and guard selected
