# Selected presentation expansion, batch 6 — 10 November 2026

Scope: 32 additional teaching points in eight further presentations. Forty-eight
of the 52 presentations now have scoped expansions. This is not a full clinical
audit, and no drug-dose tables were changed in this batch.

- Vaginal bleeding: NICE NG88 and NG12 for selected nonpregnant assessment,
  testing and referral. RCOG examination precautions distinguish digital from
  speculum examination; the former restriction is attached to possible previa.
  The source register labels the older Green-top 63 examination guidance as
  foundational; this is not a fresh audit of all obstetric recommendations.
- Nausea/vomiting: RCEM suspected CHS guideline (2024). Selected history,
  complications, haloperidol precautions and recurrence prevention. No new
  haloperidol dose or universal vomiting-treatment protocol is introduced.
- Jaundice: AASLD clinician education (2025), EASL ALF guideline (2017), ASGE
  cholangitis guideline (2021). Medication history, trajectory and staged source
  control. The education article is labelled as such, rather than as a guideline.
- Cyanosis: MHRA oximetry limitations and CDC CO guidance. Measurement quality,
  exposure clues and interpretation. No change to antidote regimens.
- Diplopia: AAO clinician education (2022). Selected associated findings,
  symptom evolution and follow-up. Existing third-nerve imaging advice and its
  separate source remain unchanged; this is not a new neuro-ophthalmology audit.
- Airway/stridor: CPS croup guidance, updated March 2026. Deterioration,
  alternative diagnoses and observation. Recurrence wording replaces rebound;
  croup discharge criteria are distinguished from other airway conditions.
- Edema: NICE NG158 and ACEP Sonoguide. The selected adult DVT assessment now
  explicitly uses one two-level probability pathway and qualifies repeat scanning
  by the DVT-likely/proximal-scan context. Limited POCUS is not treated as an
  automatic substitute for the complete diagnostic pathway. This is not a new
  review of pregnancy-related DVT diagnosis or all anticoagulant regimens.
- Falls/geriatric trauma: NICE NG249 (2025) and CG124. Multifactorial assessment,
  follow-up, injury precautions and the preferred occult-hip imaging sequence.
  This does not re-audit all head/cervical imaging criteria.
