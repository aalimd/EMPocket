# Selected presentation expansion, batch 5 — 10 November 2026

Scope: 32 additional points in eight further presentations, with selected safety
clarifications. Forty presentations now have scoped additions. This is not a full
clinical audit. Selected treatment rows change in three topics as described below.

- Weakness: EAN/PNS GBS (2023). Bulbar/cough function, serial ventilation,
  autonomic monitoring and early-test limitations. Reflexes are interpreted with
  the full pattern rather than described as a single decisive discriminator.
- Coma: accessible NCS ENLS v4 foundational assessment plus the existing stroke
  guidance. Examination trends, medication confounders and directed investigation
  when uncertainty persists. Pupil findings and ethanol levels cannot establish
  a cause alone. These are bedside principles, not a claimed audit of the newer
  ENLS v6 protocol (whose outline was located but full retrieval was restricted).
- Hemoptysis: CIRSE (2022). Contrast-protocol selection, airway priority and cause
  follow-up. CIRSE calls its document standards of practice, not a full clinical
  practice guideline; the app retains that distinction in its source title.
- Shock: SSC (2026), selected perfusion and resuscitation recommendations.
  Dynamic assessment, monitored peripheral vasopressors and reassessment replace
  categorical fluid or single-marker shortcuts. These points concern septic shock;
  management of other shock causes remains cause-directed.
- Palpitations: ISHNE/HRS (2017) and AHA ALS. Monitoring selection and correlation;
  adenosine is no longer implied as a general diagnostic trial.
- Focal neurological deficits: AHA/ASA AIS (2026), ICH (2022).
  Glucose reassessment, swallowing, transfer priority and post-EVT BP advice.
  Existing permissive-hypertension wording allows competing emergencies. ICH
  targets now specify the selected mild-to-moderate population.
- Electrolytes: UKKA (updated July 2026), KDIGO conference (2020), US sodium
  expert panel (2013). The broken UKKA link is replaced with current guidance.
  Named adult calcium regimens, closer glucose checks and prevention after insulin,
  and nonroutine bicarbonate advice replace the older abbreviated rows. The US
  sodium-bolus approach is labelled, its total count explicit, and high ODS-risk
  limits separated from initial symptom treatment. This is not a new audit of all
  calcium/magnesium/hypernatremia doses.
- Hypertensive emergencies: AHA acute-care statement (2024), ACC/AHA aorta (2022),
  AHA/ASA ICH (2022). Acute injury, individualized targets and follow-up.
  Aortic targets align with the existing chest-pain teaching; ICH scope is qualified.

New source keys: `gbs`, `comaAssessment`, `ambulatoryECG`, `acuteBP`, `ich`,
`sodiumUS`, `potassiumKDIGO`. Exact URLs, dates and scopes are in
`assets/evidence.js`. Sources were reviewed through primary publications,
accessible guideline text, society guidance and indexed primary-source extracts.

Validation: selected clinical/source regressions, search destinations, local
phone-browser rendering of all 40 expanded topics, full Node suite, syntax and
coordinated PWA release. These tests guard behavior and selected distinctions;
they do not certify all medical content.
