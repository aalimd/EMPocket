# Selected presentation expansion, batch 2 — 10 November 2026

Scope: 32 additional teaching points in eight further presentations. Together
with batch 1, 16 of 52 presentations have selected additions reviewed on this
date. This is not a full audit of those presentations or of the application.
Dose tables and the other presentations are unchanged. The additions are original
teaching synthesis using the primary sources linked in `assets/evidence.js`.

- GI bleeding: ACG UGIB (2021), ACG LGIB (2023), ASGE LGIB (2026).
  Severity and antithrombotic history; CTA for ongoing significant bleeding;
  nonurgent prepared colonoscopy for most stable inpatients; clinical judgment
  alongside scores. Existing summaries no longer imply automatic discharge from
  a low score or that a normal pulse establishes stability. The old broad
  CTA-for-obscure-bleeding line is removed in favor of the active-bleeding pathway.
- Back pain: NICE NG59 and NG234. Reassessment of changing symptoms, imaging
  decisions, MSCC escalation and advice for return. Existing six-week wording
  now clarifies that duration alone does not determine whether to image.
- Hot swollen joint: SANJO (2023), diagnostic sampling and response assessment.
  A normal inflammatory marker cannot rule out infection; diagnostic priorities
  and dry-tap management are explicit. The source concerns native joints;
  prosthetic-joint advice retains specialist referral.
- Pelvic pain: NICE NG126, pregnancy of unknown location. Diagnostic uncertainty,
  symptom-led reassessment, timed serial samples and accountable follow-up.
  The previous description of transvaginal US as definitive is qualified.
- Seizures: NICE NG217 (updated 2025), first-event assessment and selective tests.
  Witness information, EEG limitations, avoiding routine repeat CT in a typical
  established-epilepsy event, specialist referral and safety advice.
- Overdose: AHA special circumstances (2025), opioid toxicity and prevention;
  NICE NG225, psychosocial assessment and aftercare. Observation is agent- and
  course-dependent; no universal short observation window is introduced. The
  discharge line now requires completed psychosocial care when indicated.
- Hyperglycemia: adult DKA/HHS consensus (2024), monitoring, IV-to-subcutaneous
  transition and prevention of recurrence. Timings are for adult protocols;
  existing pediatric specialist-care distinctions remain.
- Flank pain: EAU Urolithiasis, diagnostic evaluation and ureteric-stone
  observation. Imaging limitations, confirming passage/renal recovery, reasons
  to escalate and how location affects passage are now explicit.

The new source keys are `ectopicDiagnosis`, `firstSeizure` and `lgibASGE`.
`topicReviews` keeps scope and supporting keys separate from the historical
2026-10-08 review date. Sources checked via official pages/search-indexed
recommendations or the primary publication. Some publishers denied full-page
retrieval; only recommendations visible in accessible primary-source text or
indexed primary-source extracts were used.

Validation covers source integrity, safety distinctions, search destinations,
phone-browser rendering of all 16 expanded topics, release consistency and the
full Node test suite. These are implementation checks, not medical certification.
