# Diagnosis guides · 13 November 2026

The first set covers all six critical Chest Pain differentials and four Abdominal Pain diagnoses. The guides focus on adult emergency recognition, initial investigation and treatment, and escalation or disposition. The PE diagnostic pathway specifies nonpregnant adults. The appendicitis guide describes the initial imaging distinction in pregnancy.

- Chest Pain. ACS, acute aortic syndrome, acute PE, tension pneumothorax, esophageal perforation and cardiac tamponade.
- Abdominal Pain. Acute mesenteric ischemia, ruptured AAA, acute appendicitis and small bowel obstruction.

The References section of each guide lists its selected sources and the scope of the check. Key sources include the 2025 ACC/AHA ACS guideline, 2022 ACC/AHA aortic disease guideline, 2026 AHA/ACC PE guideline, NICE NG39 and ACEP lung ultrasound guidance, 2025 ESC pericardial disease guidance, 2024 ESVS aneurysm guidance and WSES guidance for the surgical topics. The mesenteric ischemia guide also cites the 2025 ESVS guideline. The mesenteric ischemia and bowel obstruction guides cite the 2025 Emergency General Surgery Algorithms working group, and the tension pneumothorax and tamponade guides cite the 2025 ERC guidance on traumatic cardiac arrest.

An authored differential row has the form `[name, severity, clue, linkedTopicIds?]`. Use the optional list of topic IDs for guidance relevant to the named differential. Grouped rows can link to more than one topic. Each diagnosis guide has links back to relevant presentations under See also. The Library groups the new guides under Diagnosis guides and includes them in search.

The review covers the ten additions and the companion wording changes in Chest Pain, Abdominal Pain and Cardiac Arrest.

## 14 November expansion

Added ectopic pregnancy, ovarian torsion, testicular torsion, perforated peptic ulcer, acute cholecystitis, acute cholangitis, acute pancreatitis and acute colonic diverticulitis. Differential links also appear in related pathways including Pelvic Pain, Scrotal Pain, Jaundice, Vaginal Bleeding and Pregnancy Emergencies.

The review distinguishes the limits of hCG and Doppler imaging, gallbladder inflammation from infected bile duct obstruction, and low-risk uncomplicated diverticulitis from complications needing source control. The References section on each guide records its sources and scope. Testicular torsion includes children and adolescents. The ovarian guide focuses on adolescents and reproductive-age patients.

## 14 November dyspnea and shock expansion

Added seven adult guides for acute heart failure, acute asthma, COPD exacerbation, community-acquired pneumonia, sepsis and septic shock, cardiogenic shock, and adrenal crisis. Differential links appear in Shortness of Breath, Shock, Fever, Edema, Hypertensive Emergency and other relevant pathways.

The guidance distinguishes oxygen targets for asthma and COPD, congestion from hypoperfusion in heart failure, and immediate antibiotics for suspected septic shock or probable sepsis from time-limited assessment for possible sepsis without shock. Each guide includes the scope and source links in its References section.
