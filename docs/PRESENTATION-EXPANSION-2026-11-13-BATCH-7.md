# Final presentation expansion · 13 November 2026

The seventh batch adds 16 teaching points across the final four presentations.
All 52 presentations now have a dated record of the specific additions reviewed.
The original audit date remains the baseline for the earlier source register.

- **Major trauma.** NICE NG39 and the 2023 European trauma bleeding guideline
  inform CT and eFAST decisions, reassessment in hemorrhage, adjustment of blood
  products and cerebral perfusion in severe TBI. The initial component strategy
  follows the local hemorrhage protocol.
- **Pregnancy emergencies.** ACOG informs severe hypertension treatment, NICE
  NG126 informs follow-up for pregnancy of unknown location, and EAST informs
  observation after trauma in viable pregnancy. AHA 2025 informs preparation,
  timing and location of resuscitative delivery and maternal CPR.
- **Adult cardiac arrest.** AHA 2025 informs cardioversion energy, sustained
  polymorphic VT, treatment limits for suspected hyperkalemic arrest, opioid
  respiratory arrest versus cardiac arrest, and toxicology reassessment.
  Post-arrest teaching covers reliable oxygen measurements, selective angiography,
  and delayed multimodal neurological prognosis.
- **Thyroid emergencies.** The 2026 joint endocrine society statements and ATA
  guidelines inform clinical recognition, beta blockade precautions, intensive
  monitoring, and glucocorticoid timing and levothyroxine dosing in myxoedema coma.

Each presentation lists its primary sources in the References section. Checks
cover the corrected emergency action rows, review dates, source links, phone
viewport rendering, and the coordinated offline release.
