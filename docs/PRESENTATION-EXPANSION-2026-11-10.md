# Selected presentation expansion — 10 November 2026

Scope: 33 additional teaching points across eight adult presentations, plus
clarifications of existing investigation advice. This is a targeted content
review, not a new audit of all 52 presentations. Medication dose tables and
existing progress/preferences are unchanged.

WikEM acute chest pain and syncope pages were consulted to identify educational
gaps. The additions are original concise teaching synthesis, checked against the
primary guidance or society-authored educational material listed below. WikEM
text was not copied into the application. SAEM CDEM supports bedside assessment
principles; it is not presented as a new treatment guideline.

- Chest pain (`chest`): associated ischemic symptoms, earlier testing,
  limits of MI pathways, discharge communication and follow-up. ACC ED pathway
  (2022), sections 5.2 and 5.8.
- Dyspnea (`dyspneaAssessment`): collateral history, upper-airway warning signs,
  ventilation assessment, NIV suitability and response to initial treatment.
  SAEM CDEM Shortness of Breath, initial actions and examination.
- Abdominal pain (`abdominalAssessment`, `mesenteric`): obstruction history,
  targeted labs, repeated examination and return precautions. SAEM CDEM
  Approach to Abdominal Pain. WSES acute mesenteric ischemia guideline (2022):
  urgent CTA for suspected ischemia, specifying arterial-phase imaging rather
  than implying any routine contrast CT is sufficient. Extra-abdominal causes
  are considered alongside the abdominal differential.
- Headache (`headache`, `sah`): onset documentation, symptom relief and diagnostic
  limitations. ACEP acute headache policy (2019) allows LP or CTA for persistent
  SAH risk after a negative CT, with shared decision-making. AHA/ASA aSAH guidance
  (2023) recommends LP after negative CT for presentation beyond six hours or
  new neurological deficit. Both positions and CTA's limitations are explicit;
  the old unconditional LP sentence and oversimplified summary are replaced.
- Altered mental status (`delirium`): collateral change, hypoactive delirium,
  setting-appropriate assessment tools and supportive care. NICE CG103,
  recognition, diagnosis and management (updated 2023).
- Syncope (`syncopeGuideline`): witness recovery, selective neurological tests,
  interpreting orthostatic findings in context and choosing rhythm monitoring.
  ACC/AHA/HRS syncope guideline (2017). Seizure features trigger appropriate
  assessment, rather than implying that every suspected seizure mandates CT.
- Fever/sepsis (`sepsis`): repeated perfusion assessment, source-control needs,
  resistance history and review of empiric treatment. SSC adult guideline
  (2026), perfusion, source control and antimicrobial recommendations.
- Dizziness (`dizziness`): true triggers versus movement exacerbation,
  episodic examination, central/equivocal HINTS imaging and posterior-canal BPPV.
  SAEM GRACE-3 (2023). HINTS remains restricted to trained clinicians examining
  ongoing AVS with nystagmus.

Exact source titles and URLs are stored in `assets/evidence.js`; `topicReviews`
records the selected additions' date, scope and supporting keys. The prior
2026-10-08 review date is retained. References display both dates with explicit
scope. Existing content remains offline and the additions enter the existing
search index through their real sections.

Validation: content/source integrity and safety regression tests, targeted
browser checks of all eight updated topics and their references, and the full
Node test suite. These checks validate the implementation and selected clinical
distinctions; they do not certify every medical statement in the app.
